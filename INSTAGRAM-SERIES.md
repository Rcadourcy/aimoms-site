## PART 6 — How to actually post (so it works)

- **First 3 seconds:** already talking, looking at the lens, bold text up. No intros.
- **Caption:** repeat the hook in line 1, then 1–2 sentences, then the CTA.
- **On-screen captions:** always on (most watch on mute).
- **Post 3–4x/week**, one pain point per reel, reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **The one metric that matters:** are people **sending it to a friend?** Write for that.
- **Don't wait for perfect.** The engaging part is YOU. Press record. 🌸
