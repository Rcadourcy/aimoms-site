# 📱 Using Claude Code from your phone

Hi Raquel! This is your plain-language guide for working on the ai.moms™ site
from your phone using the **Claude app**. No terminal, no laptop needed. 💕

---

## First-time setup (do this once)

1. **Install the Claude app**
   - iPhone: open the **App Store** and search for **Claude**
   - Android: open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Claude**
   - It's the official app by **Anthropic**.

2. **Sign in with Google**
   - Use the same Google account you always use: **raquelcadourcy@gmail.com**
   - This is what connects you to your website's code.

3. **Find "Code" in the app**
   - Once you're in, look for the **Code** section.
   - You should see your project, **aimoms-site**. Tap it to open it.

That's it — you're set up. You won't have to do steps 1–2 again.

---

## Doing work from your phone (every time after that)

1. Open the **Claude** app → **Code** → **aimoms-site**.
2. Start a session and **just type what you want**, in plain words. Examples:
   - *"Change the workshop price wording on the homepage."*
   - *"Fix the typo on the newsletter signup page."*
   - *"Show me what the shop page says right now."*
3. I'll make the change and show you what I did.

You don't need to know any code or commands. Talk to me the way you would
talk to a helper who's sitting next to you.

---

## ⭐ The one thing to remember: saving your work

Your changes only become permanent once they're **saved to GitHub**
(the place your website's code lives). You don't have to do the technical part —
just tell me:

> **"Save this"** or **"Push my changes"**

…and I'll handle it. If you're ever unsure whether something was saved, just ask:

> **"Did that get saved?"**

---

## A few gentle notes

- **Each session is a fresh copy.** When you come back later, I grab a clean,
  up-to-date copy of the site. So always ask me to **save** before you close a
  session, or the change won't stick.
- **Nothing goes live automatically.** Editing here changes the *code*, not the
  live website, until we deliberately publish. So you can experiment safely.
- **No stupid questions.** If you're not sure what something means, ask. That's
  what I'm here for. You are not alone in this. 🌸
