# The ai.moms™ Community — Membership Spec
*The source of truth for what the membership includes. Last updated: 2026-07-26*

**The Community IS the membership.** One tab — "Community." A mom enters, sees what's
inside, and sees that the way in is the membership (you pay). No separate membership page.

**Flat membership:** **$29/month** or **$279/year** (pay once, ~2½ months free).

---

## Exactly what you get

### 🎥 Live sessions, every month
- **The Foundations Workshop** *(monthly)* — the reset button. A live, ground-up session so
  you're never behind, no matter when you joined.
- **Monthly Session + Office Hours** *(monthly)* — we go deep on one new thing (a workflow, a
  platform, or the AI news actually worth digging into), built around the three moms we serve
  — then we open the floor for office hours: bring what you're building, or what you're stuck on.
- **Guest Sessions & Special Workshops** *(rotating through the month)* — a changing lineup of
  AI experts, creators, and moms who've built real things. A member demoing what she made and
  walking through her process. AI safety & kids. "Bring your kid to build." A neuroscience
  deep-dive with Celia (PhD). And more.
  *(We don't commit to a fixed number — some months a big ai.moms-hosted session and a couple of
  guests; some months more. Always something worth showing up for.)*

### 💬 The always-on hub
- **Our community home on Circle** — post a question at 9pm, get an answer by morning from a mom
  who's been exactly where you are. Threads, session replays, and resources all live here.
- **Plus the WhatsApp group** — for the quick, in-the-moment stuff.

### 📍 In real life
- **City workshops & build sessions** — in-person gatherings where we learn and build together,
  popping up city by city.
- **Out of Office Weekends™** — destination weekends to learn AI and reconnect with yourself.
- **Fun things from our partners** — perks, events, and extras from the brands we love.

### ✨ Founding-member perks *(while the founding window is open)*
- **Your rate, locked** — for as long as you stay a member. The price goes up when we open fully;
  you never pay it.
- **First in the room** — before anyone else.
- **A say in what we build** — founding members shape the sessions and topics.

## Always free — and separate (NOT the membership)
These are our audience builders. They stay free, on their own pages, forever.
- **The Edit** — our publication.
- **The AI Toolkit** — reviewed tools + free mini-tools.

## Separate paid product (NOT the membership)
- **The Foundations Course** — our self-paced, on-demand course. Buy it anytime, whether or not
  you're in the community. *(Its live sibling — the monthly Foundations Workshop — lives inside
  the membership; the course is the full self-paced library. Live Stripe checkout + Make
  provisioning stay exactly as they are.)*
