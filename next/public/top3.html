Questions

What moms want to know first.

Is this coaching?

No. It's one session with a clear finish line. You bring 3 things, we get them done, and that's it. Nothing renews.

I've barely used AI. Will this work for me?

Yes, this is made for you. We start with a quick AI 101, you can ask us anything, and there are no stupid questions.

Can I use the time to just ask questions?

Yes, it's your 90 minutes. Tell us on your form or when we start, and we'll spend it however helps you most. If you choose questions over your top 3, the guarantee doesn't apply to what we didn't get to.

What if one of my 3 is too big for 90 minutes?

We read your form first. A whole website or launch can take longer than one session, so we'd agree before we meet on what done means, like a first version. If that doesn't work for you, we'll refund you.

Can I bring something from my job?

Yes. If your company has rules about AI, tell us and we'll work inside them.

What do I need?

Your laptop and a Claude account. Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant. We'll tell you which plan you need before we meet, and any Claude plan is separate from your session.

Is my information safe?

You work on your own laptop, in your own accounts. We start with your privacy settings, and we never keep your passwords. Claude recommends and you decide.

How do I pick a time?

You pick it right on this page when you book. Sessions start between 9am and 1pm ET, Monday through Friday. If none of those work, request another time and we'll work around you.

Can I bring my partner or a friend?

Your partner is welcome, especially for anything you share, like the family calendar. For a group of friends, our in-person experiences are a better fit.

I already bought the Foundations course.

Then you're a great fit. We'll pick up wherever you stopped and get it working on your real week.

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem. Just tell us at least 24 hours before your session.
