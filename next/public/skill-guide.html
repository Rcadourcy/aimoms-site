The Skill Guide · 25 skills · 3 worlds

Every skill in the ai.moms™ plugin, and when to use it.

Type /aimoms in Claude to see them all, or just say the job in plain words and Aime, your AI Chief Everything Officer™, picks the right one.

1

Add the plugin

In Claude: Customize → Plugins → + → Add plugin → choose the file. Don't unzip it.

2

Meet Aime

Create a project named Aime, Chief Everything Officer, open a chat and type /aimoms-start.

3

Run a skill

Type /aimoms, or tell Aime what you need. Then ask her to put it on a schedule.

Runs your world

Start here
/aimoms-start

Create a new project, name it Aime, Chief Everything Officer. Open a new chat inside it and type /aimoms-start. Aime introduces herself, explains how the plugin works, and runs her get-to-know-your-family interview (45 to 60 minutes, pause anytime, or skip it for now). Your family report saves right into the project, and Aime hands you her instructions to paste into the project's instructions, one copy-paste and you're done. Every chat after this one starts with her knowing your family.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: Your family report saved in the project, plus the Aime instructions to paste in, one step and done
/aimoms-brain-dump

Got a million things in your head? Say them all, in any order. Aime sorts everything into a to-do list that's saved in your project, tells you the three that matter most, and nothing gets dropped, because it lives on paper now instead of in your head.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: A sorted to-do list saved in your project, plus an optional Family Dashboard
/aimoms-weekly-plan

Plans your whole week ahead, run on any day you like: every schedule conflict spotted with a fix, dinners picked with recipes and one grocery list, upcoming birthdays caught with gift ideas, and everything to buy in one list you approve once. Your weekend stays free.

Uses: Google Calendar, Gmail  ·  You get: Your weekly brief in the format you choose, plus one shopping list to approve
/aimoms-daily-brief

Your morning rundown. Today's meetings and kid logistics on one page, your top three for the day, and anything colliding (the 4pm call vs. the 4:15 pickup) with the fix already drafted. Also checks your inbox for anything that should be on today's calendar but isn't.

Uses: Google Calendar, Gmail  ·  You get: A one-page brief for today
/aimoms-friday-shutdown

Run it Friday afternoon. Aime shows what you finished, what moves to next week, and what you're allowed to drop, then drafts the two-minute replies you owe, so the weekend starts with a clear head.

Uses: Gmail, Google Calendar  ·  You get: Three short lists (done, moves, drop) plus drafted replies ready to send
/aimoms-find-me-time

Shows where your week's hours actually go, finds the ones you can get back, and puts one block on the calendar that is just for you.

Uses: Google Calendar  ·  You get: Recovered hours and one protected block on your calendar
/aimoms-guilt-check

For the moment the mom guilt starts. Aime helps you sort what truly needs attention from what's just impossible standards, and hands you one sentence to move forward with. Three short paragraphs, never a lecture.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: A short reset and one next step
/aimoms-build-assistant

Have a job that comes up over and over, like class-rep emails or your side business? Aime interviews you about it, then builds you a dedicated assistant: the instructions, the starter prompts, and the setup steps.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: A complete assistant package, ready to paste into a new Claude Project
/aimoms-sop

Turns anything you do on repeat (the school-morning routine, sitter night, packing for ski weekends) into a one-page playbook anyone can follow, so the job can finally leave your head.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: A one-page playbook saved in your project, with sitter and kid versions on request

Runs your home

/aimoms-school-digest

Paste or screenshot the school emails and app posts. Aime pulls out only what needs action: dates, forms, money owed, RSVPs, with anything due in 48 hours on top. Everything else gets two lines.

Uses: Gmail, Google Calendar  ·  You get: One action list, plus calendar entries and replies staged for your okay
/aimoms-weekly-dinner-plan

Plans the week's dinners around your real schedule: quick meals on busy nights, simple recipes, and one grocery list in the order you walk your store.

Uses: Google Calendar  ·  You get: The week's dinners, recipes, and one store-ordered grocery list
/aimoms-emergency-dinner

It's 5:30 and there's no plan. Snap a photo of your fridge and get two or three real dinners you can make in 30 minutes or less, fastest first.

Uses: Nothing to connect, just a photo  ·  You get: Two or three dinner options with steps, fastest first
/aimoms-sick-day

A kid wakes up sick. Aime drafts the school absence email and your work messages, rearranges your day, and plans the couch-and-soup part, all in one go.

Uses: Gmail, Google Calendar  ·  You get: The absence email, your work messages, and your rearranged day
/aimoms-school-breaks

School's closed but work isn't. Aime maps every day off, researches real local break camps with prices and hours, and helps line up swaps and coverage for each day.

Uses: Google Calendar, web research  ·  You get: A day-by-day coverage plan and a camp comparison table
/aimoms-summer-camp

Plans the entire summer: asks your kids what they loved last year, researches real camps, watches registration deadlines so you never hit a waitlist, and builds a week-by-week plan you can see as a spreadsheet, calendar, or dashboard and share with the family.

Uses: Google Calendar, Gmail, web research  ·  You get: A living summer plan in the format you pick, plus registrations prepped for your okay
/aimoms-celebration

Birthdays and holidays from start to finish: theme, invitations, menu, shopping lists, games, the hour-by-hour day-of plan, goodie bags, and cleanup. Plus a year-round gift ledger so you never repeat a gift.

Uses: Canva, Google Calendar, web research  ·  You get: The full party plan: run of show, shopping lists, invitations, goodie bags
/aimoms-activities

"What should we do this weekend?" Aime finds what's actually happening near you, matched to your kids' ages and your free hours, with times, costs, and the free options first.

Uses: Google Calendar, web research  ·  You get: Three to five weekend picks with times, costs, and drive times
/aimoms-travel-plan

Plans the family trip: where to go, the countdown checklist with passports flagged early, and packing lists so clear your partner or kid can pack from them.

Uses: Google Calendar  ·  You get: The trip plan, countdown checklist, and per-person packing lists
/aimoms-fair-split

Makes the invisible work visible. Aime lists everything you actually carry, helps split it so every task has one real owner, and drafts the conversation with your partner.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: The full task list, a proposed split, and the conversation drafted
/aimoms-village-ask

Drafts the asks that are easy to say yes to: the carpool text, the meal train message, the emergency backup plan. And keeps track of whose kid you owe a pickup.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: Copy-paste-ready asks and a favor tracker

Runs your career

/aimoms-meeting-prep

Ten minutes before the meeting: your goal, who's in the room and what they care about, the three points to land, and answers ready for the pushback you'll probably get.

Uses: Gmail, Google Drive  ·  You get: A prep sheet that fits on your phone
/aimoms-inbox-triage

Cuts the work inbox down to size: what needs your decision, what gets a quick reply (already drafted in your voice), what can wait, and the one email that matters most today.

Uses: Gmail  ·  You get: A sorted inbox and replies drafted for your okay
/aimoms-boundary-draft

The no you've been rewriting in your head, drafted three ways: warm, neutral, and firm. Short, confident, zero over-apologizing.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: Three ready-to-send versions; you pick the tone
/aimoms-career-wins

Mention a work win and Aime logs it with the numbers. By review season you have a year of receipts instead of a blank page.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: Your running wins file, saved in your project
/aimoms-review-prep

Turns your logged wins into the self-assessment, the promotion case, or interview answers, and rehearses the conversation with you.

Uses: Nothing to connect  ·  You get: Your self-assessment or promotion case, plus out-loud rehearsal
