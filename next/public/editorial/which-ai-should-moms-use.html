Which AI Should Moms Use? How to Get Started in Claude

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, NotebookLM: what each one is good for, plus how to get started in Claude, from the screen to your memory.

By The ai.moms™ teamNovember 12, 20264 min read

Key Takeaways

  • Each AI has a job it does well. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and NotebookLM are each good at something different.
  • Pick one home base and learn it well. We teach on Claude because it’s where Aime and the ai.moms™ plugin live.
  • Five spots on the Claude screen are all you need. New chat, the chat box, the + button, the mic and Projects.
  • Start with the default model. You can switch models anytime from the menu under the chat box.
  • You don’t have to start over. If you’ve used ChatGPT, you can bring what it knows about your family into Claude.

“Which AI should I use?” is the question we get most, at every workshop, in every DM. It usually comes from a mom who has three apps downloaded, used each one twice and isn’t sure any of them is the right one.

That uncertainty keeps a lot of moms stuck at the starting line. The school forms keep coming, the dinner question keeps coming, and the AI app sits on the second screen of the phone, unopened.

So this is the short version: what each tool is good for, which one we teach on and how to get started so the second time is easy.

Which AI Should Moms Use?

The short answer: pick one home base and learn it well, then add other tools for specific jobs. At ai.moms™ our home base is Claude, and the prompting skills you learn there work in ChatGPT, Gemini and the rest.

You don’t need all five. Most moms do almost everything in one, and reach for another when a job calls for it.

Why Do We Teach Moms to Learn AI in Claude?

We teach moms who want to learn AI in Claude because it handles the full range of what moms actually need, from a quick rewrite to an assistant that remembers your family. It’s also where Aime, your AI Chief Everything Officer™, and the 25 skills in the ai.moms™ plugin run. If you already love another tool, keep it. The habits you build here carry over.

How Do Moms Get Started in Claude?

Step 1: Change three settings first

Before you tell Claude anything about your family, take about a minute in Settings. Turn off the option that lets your chats help train the models, and turn on the memory options so it remembers your family. We walk through each one in AI Privacy for Moms.

Step 2: Learn five spots on the screen

  1. New chat: one topic, one chat. The dinner plan and the work email get separate chats.
  2. The chat box: full sentences about your real situation.
  3. The + button: add photos, files and screenshots, like the school flyer or a picture of your fridge.
  4. The mic: don’t type, talk. Perfect for the carpool line.
  5. Projects: where Claude remembers you. More on this in Claude Projects for Moms.

Step 3: Pick your model

Same Claude, a few different models, and the menu is right under the chat box. Opus is the everyday driver for almost everything. Sonnet and Haiku are quicker for a rephrase or summing up a long email, and they make your plan go further. Fable is the newest and most powerful, for your toughest problem. Start with Opus. You can change it anytime.

Step 4: Bring your ChatGPT memory over

Switching from ChatGPT? Everything it knows about your family can come with you.

  1. In Claude, go to Settings → Memory → Start import.
  2. Copy the prompt Claude gives you and paste it into ChatGPT.
  3. Paste ChatGPT’s answer back into Claude.
  4. Choose Add to memory, and read what it saved.

Step 5: Hand it one real task

Skip the test questions. Give it something from today’s list, using the prompt below.

What Should You Ask Claude First?

This one helps you decide where AI can help you first, so your first week has a plan.

Your first prompt

Role: You are a friendly AI coach who helps busy moms start using Claude in real life.

Context: I’m a [mom at home / mom at work / mompreneur] with [insert number] kids, ages [insert ages]. The weekly tasks that take the most of my time and headspace are [insert three or four, like dinner planning, school emails, work follow-ups or client invoices]. I’m [new to AI / I’ve used ChatGPT a little].

Task: Pick the one task where Claude would help me most this week, and show me exactly how to hand it over. Then suggest one small new thing to try each day for the next five days.

Format: A short plan: the first task with a ready-to-use prompt, then a five-day list with one line per day.

Before you start, ask me any clarifying questions.

What Should Moms Double-Check?

  • Read what memory saved. After an import, look over the list and delete anything you don’t want stored.
  • Free or paid is your call. You can start on a free account and decide later if you need more. Plans and features change, so check the current options in the app.
  • Any tool can be wrong. Whichever one you use, check dates, prices and anything medical, legal or money-related.

What’s the Right Setup for Each Mom?

  • Mom at home: Claude as home base, NotebookLM for the school handbook, the mic for everything in the car.
  • Mom at work: check what your company allows at work, and keep a separate personal account for family life.
  • Mompreneur: Claude for your brand voice and content, Perplexity when you need sources you can cite.

Getting Started With AI: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI for moms?

The best one is the one you’ll use every day. We teach on Claude because it handles writing, planning and assistants that remember your family, and it’s where Aime and the ai.moms™ plugin run. ChatGPT and Gemini are good choices too.

Is Claude better than ChatGPT?

They’re both strong, and the same prompting skills work in each. We use Claude as our home base, and you can bring your ChatGPT memory over if you switch.

Which Claude model should I use?

Start with Opus for everyday tasks. Use Sonnet or Haiku for quick rewrites and summaries, and Fable for your hardest problems. You can change models from the menu under the chat box.

Can I move my ChatGPT history to Claude?

You can bring over what ChatGPT remembers about you. In Claude, go to Settings → Memory → Start import and follow the steps. Your old chats stay in ChatGPT.

How Can Moms Start Using AI Tonight?

Maybe you’re the mom at home with three AI apps you haven’t opened, the mom at work who wants to stop feeling behind, or the mompreneur who needs a home base for her business. Getting started looks the same for each of you: three settings, five spots on the screen and one real task.

Once that feels easy, make it remember you with a Project, and keep your family’s details safe while you do it. Both are next in our Foundations Course.
