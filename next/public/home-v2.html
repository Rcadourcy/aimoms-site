Questions

Before you start.

I'm not technical. Can I actually do this?

Yes. We walk you through every single click. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this. We designed this for busy moms, not engineers.

How is this different from just asking AI questions on my own?

Most people use AI like a search engine: ask a question, get a generic answer. We teach you the tool from the ground up, show you real demos for home, work and business, and you build Aime, an assistant that knows your family, your preferences and your schedule.

How long does it take?

Each lesson is 5 to 10 minutes. You can finish the whole course in a week or spread it over a month. Watch whenever works for you: nap time, after bedtime, lunch break.

Is this just another productivity hack?

No. You're learning a complete AI skill set: effective prompting, custom assistants and real automated systems for your household and work. This is infrastructure, not tips.

Is my family's information safe?

We teach you privacy best practices and how to use AI platforms safely, so you stay in control of what you share.

Can my partner join too?

The course is designed for the primary household manager (usually mom), but you're welcome to share access with your partner. The skills benefit the whole family.

Which AI do I need?

Aime and her skills run on Claude. We'll show you exactly how to set it up and which plan you need.

What are the payment options?

The Foundations Course is $247 on demand and includes all 6 modules, Aime onboarding and all 25 skills. Prefer to learn in a room? Join us in person for $265, with the course included.
